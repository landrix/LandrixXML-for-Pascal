## Apache Xerces

The XML Schema units (`source/LandrixXmlXsd*.pas`) reproduce the behaviour
of Apache Xerces-J 2.12.2 as shipped in the `java.xml` module of the Java
runtime: they report its error codes (`cvc-...`) and messages in its
wording, and the identity constraint matching in
`LandrixXmlXsdValidator.pas` is a port of its `XPathMatcher` and value
stores (`ValueStoreBase`, `KeyRefValueStore`, `ValueStoreCache`). The
XML Schema half of `source/LandrixXmlBlockData.inc` (the block names its
regular expressions accept) is computed from its behaviour. The
expected results in `tests/data/xsdtypes/`, `tests/data/xsdcontent/` and
`tests/data/xsdvalidator/` are output of that validator. Apache Xerces is

## Saxon

`source/LandrixXmlCaseData.inc` (the results of `fn:upper-case` and
`fn:lower-case` per XML character) is generated by
`tools/gen-xpath-case-tables.py`, and
`source/LandrixXmlCaseVariantsData.inc` (the case variants of the regular
expression flag `i`) by `tools/gen-regex-case-variants.py`, and the XPath
half of `source/LandrixXmlBlockData.inc` by `tools/gen-regex-blocks.py`,
from the output of SaxonJ-HE 12.9 (Saxonica, Mozilla Public License 2.0,
https://www.saxonica.com/). They contain no Saxon code or data files,
only the computed results; the case variants and blocks found this way
are the same mappings as Saxon's `casevariants.xml` and
`unicodeBlocks.xml` (blocks: on XML characters).
